The reality we are changing

The Story of Esther & Millions of Adolescent Girls

In Makoko, Yaba, Lagos, young girls like Esther routinely miss school during their periods because they cannot afford commercial sanitary products.

1/10

School absenteeism. UNESCO reports 1 in 10 Sub-Saharan African girls miss school during their cycle, losing up to 20% of the school year.

37M

Lack of access. Over 37 million Nigerian women and girls lack hygienic products because of high inflation and costs.

250+

Plastic waste. Standard pads contain plastic that takes 250 to 800 years to decompose.
